# Users

Users define who can operate, review, and administer Orlo within an organization.

This surface matters because responsible AI operation requires named owners, reviewers, and administrators.

## Who Uses It

- organization administrators
- platform owners
- governance leads
- security teams
- business owners assigning reviewers

## What It Shows

The Users surface should show:

- user identity
- organization membership
- role or access level
- reviewer responsibilities
- administrative status
- access changes

## Why It Matters

AI accountability should not be anonymous.

Users, roles, and reviewer assignments connect runtime evidence to the people responsible for operating and reviewing the system.
