# Mission Control

Mission Control is the starting point for live Orlo operations.

It helps platform teams and AI owners understand the current state of the control plane without jumping directly into API logs.

## Who Uses It

- platform leaders tracking AI adoption
- AI product owners watching live deployments
- operations teams triaging failures and review pressure
- risk and compliance teams checking whether evidence exists

## What It Shows

Mission Control should orient teams around:

- active tasks and deployments
- recent evaluations
- validation failures
- approval queues
- monitoring alerts
- feedback pressure
- agent sessions that need review
- integration health

## Why It Matters

Mission Control makes Orlo visible as a product surface, not just an API.

It answers the first operational question: what is running, what changed, and what needs attention?
